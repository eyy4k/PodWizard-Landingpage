## 1. INTRODUCTION

This Data Processing Addendum (DPA) supplements the Privacy Policy and sets out the terms and conditions for the processing of personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), the UK Data Protection Act 2018, and other applicable data protection laws.

**Key Definitions:**
- **Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person
- **Processing:** Any operation performed on personal data (collection, use, storage, deletion, etc.)
- **Data Controller:** PodWizard (determines purposes and means of processing)
- **Data Processor:** Third parties acting on behalf of PodWizard (Stripe, Google, AWS, etc.)
- **Data Subject:** You (the person whose data is being processed)

## 3. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

PodWizard processes personal data under the following legal bases (GDPR Article 6):

**1. Performance of Contract (Article 6(1)(b))**
- Account creation and management
- Service delivery
- Payment processing
- Content storage and generation

**2. Legitimate Interest (Article 6(1)(f))**
- Service improvement and analytics
- Security and fraud prevention
- Marketing communications (with opt-out option)

**3. Consent (Article 6(1)(a))**
- Non-essential cookies
- Marketing emails
- Analytics tracking
- Third-party integrations

**4. Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c))**
- Payment record retention (tax laws)
- Abuse reporting (law enforcement)
- Data breach notification

### 3.2 Special Category Data

PodWizard **does not intentionally collect** special category data (race, ethnicity, health, political views, etc.). If such data is inadvertently included in user-generated content, it will be treated with heightened protection.

## 6. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

### 6.1 Your Privacy Rights (GDPR Articles 12-22)

As a data subject, you have the following rights:

**1. Right of Access (Article 15)**
- Request a copy of all personal data we hold
- Learn how your data is processed
- Response time: 30 days

**2. Right to Rectification (Article 16)**
- Correct inaccurate personal data
- Complete incomplete data
- No time limit

**3. Right to Erasure (Article 17) - "Right to be Forgotten"**
- Request deletion of your data
- Request deletion of publicly available data
- Exceptions: legal obligations, legitimate interests
- Response time: 30 days

**4. Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18)**
- Limit how your data is used
- Useful if accuracy is disputed
- Response time: immediately

**5. Right to Data Portability (Article 20)**
- Receive your data in machine-readable format
- Transfer data to another service
- Response time: 30 days

**6. Right to Object (Article 21)**
- Object to marketing communications
- Object to processing for legitimate interests
- Object to automated decision-making
- Response time: 30 days

**7. Right Related to Automated Decision-Making (Article 22)**
- The right NOT to be subject to profiling
- Exceptions: necessary for contract performance, with consent, or legal obligation
- Request human review of decisions

### 6.2 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact:

**Email:** privacy@podwizard.com  
**Post:** PodWizard Inc., Privacy Department, [Address]  
**Online Form:** podwizard.com/data-subject-rights

**Required Information:**
- Full name

## 7. DATA SECURITY & PROTECTION

### 7.1 Security Measures

PodWizard implements the following security controls:

**Technical Measures:**
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Password hashing (bcrypt)
- Regular penetration testing
- Intrusion detection systems
- Web Application Firewall (WAF)

**Organizational Measures:**
- Access control (role-based permissions)
- Employee data protection training
- Confidentiality agreements with staff
- Background checks for relevant personnel
- Incident response procedures
- Regular security audits

**Physical Measures:**
- Secured data centers (AWS)
- CCTV and access controls
- Fire suppression systems
- Redundant power supplies

### 7.2 Data Breach Response

In the event of a data breach, PodWizard will:

1. **Investigate** within 72 hours
2. **Assess** risk to your data and rights
3. **Notify** supervisory authority if high risk (within 72 hours)
4. **Inform** affected individuals (without undue delay)
5. **Provide** information on:
   - Nature of the breach
   - Data controller contact
   - Likely consequences
   - Measures taken to address breach
   - Recommendations to protect yourself
