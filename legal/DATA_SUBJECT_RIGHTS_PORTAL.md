## 1. OVERVIEW OF YOUR RIGHTS

As a user of PodWizard, you have fundamental rights regarding your personal data. These rights differ based on where you live:

### ✅ Available to ALL Users

| Right | What It Means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Right of Access** | You can request all data we hold about you |
| **Right to Know Data Sources** | You can ask where your data came from |
| **Request Correction** | You can fix inaccurate information |
| **Request Deletion** | You can ask us to delete your data |
| **Data Portability** | You can get your data in a standard format |
| **Opt-Out of Marketing** | You can stop marketing communications |
| **Lodge a Complaint** | You can report to privacy authorities |

### 🇪🇺 GDPR Rights (EU, EEA, UK Residents)

- Right of Access (Article 15)
- Right to Rectification (Article 16)
- Right to Erasure (Article 17)
- Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18)
- Right to Data Portability (Article 20)
- Right to Object (Article 21)
- Right Against Automated Decision-Making (Article 22)
- Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 7)

### 🇺🇸 CCPA Rights (California Residents)

- Right to Know (request collection disclosure)
- Right to Access (receive all personal information)
- Right to Delete (request data deletion)
- Right to Correct (fix inaccurate data)
- Right to Opt-Out (stop data sales - we don't sell data)
- Right to Limit Use (restrict data usage)
- Right to Non-Discrimination (equal service for exercising rights)

### 🇧🇷 LGPD Rights (Brazilian Residents)

- Right of Access to information
- Right to Know Data Origins
- Right to Data Correction
- Right to Data Deletion
- Right to Data Portability
- Right to Withdraw Consent
- Right to Request Non-Use of Data
- Right to Contest Decisions

### 🇨🇦 PIPEDA Rights (Canadian Residents)

- Right to Access Personal Information
- Right to Request Correction
- Right to Withdraw Consent
- Right to Challenge Accuracy
- Right to File Complaint

## 2. RIGHT OF ACCESS (GDPR Art. 15 / CCPA § 1798.100)

### What Is It?

You can request a copy of ALL personal data we have about you.

### What Will You Receive?

- Complete account information
- Email address and contact details
- All content you've created (episode data, scripts, notes)
- Your payment and subscription history
- Usage logs and analytics data
- Communication history
- All records in a standard, machine-readable format
- A list of all third parties with access to your data

### How to Request

**Online:**
1. Visit: podwizard.com/data-subject-rights
2. Click "Request Data Access"
3. Verify your identity
4. Submit request

**Email:**
Send to: privacy@podwizard.com  
Subject: "Data Access Request - GDPR Article 15"

Include:
- Full name
- Email address
- Account ID (if available)
- Date range (if specific)

### Response Time

**GDPR:** 30 days  
**CCPA:** 45 days  
**LGPD:** 15 days (renewable once)  
**Standard:** No later than 30 days

### Format Options

- **JSON:** Machine-readable format (recommended)
- **CSV:** Spreadsheet format
- **PDF:** Human-readable document
- **Printed:** Physical copy (may incur postage)

## 3. RIGHT TO RECTIFICATION (GDPR Art. 16)

### What Is It?

You can correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

### Examples

- Spelling errors in your name
- Outdated email address
- Incorrect billing information
- Outdated subscription details
- Wrong podcast information

- Details of incorrect data
- Correct information
- Supporting documentation (if applicable)

### Response Time

Immediately (self-service)  
7 days (formal request)

## 4. RIGHT TO ERASURE (GDPR Art. 17 / CCPA § 1798.105)

### What Is It?

You can request deletion of your personal data (the "Right to be Forgotten").

### What Gets Deleted

✅ **Will be deleted:**
- Your account and all account data
- Episode content and scripts
- User preferences and settings
- Analytics and usage data
- Email address and contact info
- Payment records (after legal retention period)

❌ **Will NOT be deleted:**
- Payment records (required by tax law for 7 years)
- If necessary for legal proceedings
- If required by law enforcement
- Anonymized/aggregated data (not identifiable as you)
- Backup copies (deleted within 30 days)

### When We Cannot Delete

- If required by law (tax, legal holds)
- If content is essential to fulfill contract
- If deletion violates another user's rights
- If data is needed for fraud prevention

### How to Request

**Online:**
1. Visit: podwizard.com/data-subject-rights
2. Click "Request Data Deletion"
3. Confirm your identity
4. Confirm you understand the consequences
5. Submit request

**Email:**
Send to: privacy@podwizard.com  
Subject: "Data Erasure Request - GDPR Article 17"

Include:
- Full name
- Email address
- Account ID
- Reason for deletion (optional)

### Response Time

**GDPR:** 30 days  
**CCPA:** 45 days  
**LGPD:** 15 days

### After Deletion

- Account is permanently closed
- All data deleted within 30 days
- Backups deleted within 30 days
- You receive deletion confirmation
- Cannot be undone

## 5. RIGHT TO RESTRICT PROCESSING (GDPR Art. 18)

### What Is It?

You can limit how your data is used while we review its accuracy or legality.

### When to Use

- You dispute the accuracy of data
- You believe processing is unlawful
- You want to preserve data for legal claim
- You've objected to processing

### What Happens

- Data is kept but not used
- Processing is paused (except storage)
- Restrictions remain until issue is resolved
- You can request removal of restrictions

### How to Request

Email: privacy@podwizard.com  
Subject: "Request to Restrict Processing"

Include:
- Reason for restriction
- Which data should be restricted
- Expected duration

## 6. RIGHT TO DATA PORTABILITY (GDPR Art. 20 / CCPA § 1798.100)

### What Is It?

You can receive your data in a standard format and transfer it to another service.

### What's Included

✅ Portable data:
- Account information
- Episode metadata
- Scripts and content (your creations)
- User preferences
- Listening/usage history

### What's NOT Portable

❌ Non-portable data:
- Inferred or derived data
- Analytics and profiling
- Third-party data
- Aggregated data

### How to Request

**Online:**
1. Visit: podwizard.com/data-subject-rights
2. Click "Request Data Portability"
3. Select format (JSON, CSV, XML)
4. Submit request

**Email:**
Subject: "Data Portability Request - GDPR Article 20"

### Supported Formats

| Format | Best For | Example |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **JSON** | Technical systems | Structured data, APIs |
| **CSV** | Spreadsheets | Excel, Google Sheets |
| **XML** | Data interchange | System-to-system transfer |
| **PDF** | Human reading | Printable format |

### Response Time

30 days

## 7. RIGHT TO OBJECT (GDPR Art. 21 / CCPA § 1798.120)

### What Is It?

You can object to certain types of data processing.

### When to Use

**Object to Marketing:**
- Stop marketing emails immediately
- Click "Unsubscribe" in email footer (instant)
- Or request formal opt-out

**Object to Analytics:**
- Stop usage tracking
- Request opt-out from Google Analytics

**Object to Legitimate Interest:**
- Disagree with processing based on our interests
- Request processing to stop

### How to Request

**Marketing Emails:**
Click the "Unsubscribe" link in any email (immediate)

**Analytics:**
Reject analytics cookies in cookie management panel

**Formal Objection:**
Email: privacy@podwizard.com  
Subject: "Objection to Processing"

Include:
- Which processing you're objecting to
- Reason for objection
- Which rights this relates to

### Response Time

Immediate (marketing)  
30 days (other processing)

## 8. RIGHT AGAINST AUTOMATED DECISION-MAKING (GDPR Art. 22)

### What Is It?

You have the right NOT to be subject to decisions made solely by automated means (like profiling).

### What PodWizard Does

✅ **We do NOT use automated decision-making for:**
- Denying service
- Pricing decisions
- Subscription recommendations
- Content recommendations
- Account suspension

⚠️ **We use AI/automation for:**
- Episode generation (with your control)
- Spam/abuse detection (with human review)
- Personalized feature suggestions (with opt-out)

### Your Rights

- Right to know when automation affects you
- Right to human review of decisions
- Right to contest automated decisions
- Right to explanation of how decision was made

### How to Request

If you believe we're making decisions about you using only automation:

Email: privacy@podwizard.com  
Subject: "Automated Decision Review Request"

Include:
- Which decision you're challenging
- Why you believe it's automated
- Request for human review

## 9. WITHDRAW CONSENT (GDPR Art. 7)

### What Is It?

You can withdraw consent you've previously given for specific processing.

### Examples of Consent You Can Withdraw

- Marketing emails
- Analytics cookies
- Social media integration
- Optional features
- Newsletter subscriptions

### You CANNOT Withdraw Consent For

- Essential cookies (service won't work)
- Core service functionality
- Legal/compliance obligations

### How to Withdraw Consent

**Cookies:**
Go to: podwizard.com/cookie-management  
Toggle off the categories you don't consent to

**Marketing:**
Click "Unsubscribe" in any email (immediate)

**Services:**
Email: privacy@podwizard.com  
Subject: "Withdraw Consent"

Include:
- Specific consent you're withdrawing
- Effective date (can be immediate)

### Effect

- Processing stops immediately
- Withdrawal is irreversible
- Past processing remains lawful
- You can re-consent anytime

## 11. RESPONSE TIMES & PROCESS

### Standard Timeline

**Step 1: Receipt (1 day)**
- Acknowledge request receipt
- Provide reference number
- Confirm what we received

**Step 2: Verification (3-5 days)**
- Verify your identity
- Confirm it's your data
- May request additional information

**Step 3: Processing (15-25 days)**
- Locate your data
- Prepare response
- Ensure accuracy

**Step 4: Response (30 days)**
- Deliver data/action
- Provide explanation if we're refusing
- Give appeal information

### Extensions

We may extend response time by **2 months** if:
- Request is extremely complex
- Multiple requests received
- Request is manifestly unfounded

You'll be notified of extension with explanation.

### If We Refuse Your Request

We may refuse if:
- Request is manifestly unfounded
- Request is frivolous or excessive
- Exception applies (legal, etc.)

**If we refuse, you will receive:**
- Written explanation
- Reasons for refusal
- Information about legal remedies
- Contact for complaints

## 12. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Q: Do these rights cost anything?
**A:** No. First request per year is free. Subsequent requests may incur a reasonable fee to cover administrative costs.

### Q: How long does it take to delete my data?
**A:** Account and personal data: 30 days. Payment records: 7 years (legal requirement). Backups: 30 days.

### Q: Can I request my data for someone else?
**A:** No, unless you have legal authority (power of attorney, guardianship). We'll verify legal documents.

### Q: What if I delete my account but want my data later?
**A:** Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered. Make sure you download/export your data first.

### Q: Do you sell my data?
**A:** No. PodWizard does not sell, trade, or rent personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

### Q: Can I still use PodWizard after requesting deletion?
**A:** Yes, but your old account is deleted. You can create a new account with a different email.

### Q: What if you're located outside my country?
**A:** We comply with all applicable laws in your jurisdiction. Your rights are protected by law regardless of our location.

### Q: How do I appeal if you refuse my request?
**A:** You can:
1. Appeal to us in writing (privacy@podwizard.com)
2. Contact your supervisory authority
3. Pursue legal action in court

### Q: What's the difference between delete and restrict?
**A:** 
- **Restrict:** Data stays but isn't used (temporary)
- **Delete:** Data is permanently removed (permanent)

### Q: Can I export just my episodes?
**A:** Yes. Use Data Portability request and select "Episodes Only" to receive just your content files.

### Q: What happens to my data if PodWizard shuts down?
**A:** We have a Data Exit Protocol ensuring:
- 90 days notice to all users
- Free data export for all users
- Secure deletion after retention periods
- Third-party data return to users
